How a mover gets found

Subtract one epoch from the other.

The detector looks for anything that changed position or brightness between two observations of the same field.

  1. 01

    Subtract

    Take epoch B minus epoch A. Stars that stayed put cancel out.

  2. 02

    Estimate the noise

    Measure it with the median absolute deviation, so a few bright sources do not skew it.

  3. 03

    Flag peaks

    Keep peaks above the threshold, 5 sigma by default. A bright and a dark peak within 25 pixels count as one moving source.

  4. 04

    Centroid

    Re-measure each position by centroiding to get the displacement.

A change of less than 10% of a source's brightness is rated Low reliability.

Inside the app

Five views of one sky.

Start with the sky, then move through time, candidates and colour.

  • Sky Viewer

    Zoom and pan, read RA and Dec, and click markers.

  • Time Machine

    Side by side, blink, swipe, difference and a timeline.

  • Candidate Explorer

    Positions in A and B, displacement, significance and expected false alarms, with a High, Medium or Low reliability label.

  • Spectral Explorer

    The same field in six bands, plus a model spectrum across 102 bands.

  • About & Data

    Methods, data sources, validation and limitations.

Validation and limits

Tested on simulated skies.

  • Candidates are automatic detections and are not verified. Check target coordinates in SIMBAD.
  • The simulated sky does not match the real sky at the coordinates you enter, and its fast mover is exaggerated.
  • Compare observations at about the same wavelength only.
  • Target coordinates and proper motions are approximate.
